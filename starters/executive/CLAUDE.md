# Executive Strategy Vault

This is a Context Nest vault for executive leadership — strategy, operations, and decision-making.

## What This Vault Is For

You are working with a senior leader who uses this vault to:
- Organize strategic thinking and operational frameworks
- Prepare for board meetings, leadership discussions, and alignment conversations
- Build and refine talking points, decision frameworks, and playbooks
- Track strategic initiatives and their rationale

## Vault Structure

- `nodes/strategy/` — Strategic plans, market positioning, competitive analysis, initiative proposals
- `nodes/operations/` — Operational frameworks, process documentation, governance models, KPIs
- `nodes/leadership/` — Talking points, alignment playbooks, stakeholder communication, team development

## How to Use Context Nest Commands

- `ctx search <query>` — Find specific content across the vault
- `ctx resolve <selector>` — Query by tags, type, or status (supports AND/OR/NOT)
- `ctx add <path>` — Create a new document
- `ctx update <path>` — Modify an existing document
- `ctx verify` — Validate integrity of all documents
- `ctx history <path>` — View version timeline of a document

## First 5 Things To Do

1. **Read the existing documents** in `nodes/` to understand what templates are here
2. **Ask the user about their current priorities** — what strategic decisions are they facing?
3. **Help them fill in the strategy template** with their specific context — company, market, goals
4. **Create a talking points document** for their most pressing upcoming conversation
5. **Suggest additional documents** that would make this vault more useful for their role

## Working Style

- Be direct and concise — executives don't have time for fluff
- Frame recommendations in terms of business outcomes
- When creating documents, use the YAML frontmatter format (title, type, tags, priority, status)
- Always ask before creating new documents — suggest first, then create with approval
