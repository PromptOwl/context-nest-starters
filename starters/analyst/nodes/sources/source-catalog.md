## Reliability Assessment Criteria

| Rating | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **High** | Primary source, verified data, consistent track record |
| **Medium** | Secondary source, generally reliable, occasional errors |
| **Low** | Unverified, crowdsourced, or known to have gaps |
